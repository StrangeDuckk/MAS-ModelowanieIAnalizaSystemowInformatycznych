ASSOCIATIONS
------------

1. Company --- JobOffer
   type: Composition
   role: "Announces"
   multiplicity:
     Company: 1
     JobOffer: 0..*

   meaning:
     Company announces JobOffers.
     JobOffer cannot exist without Company.


2. Company --- ComAdr
   role: "Located in"
   multiplicity:
     Company: 1
     ComAdr: 0..*

   meaning:
     Company was at ComAdr from x till y time.


3. ComAdr --- Address
   multiplicity:
     ComAdr: 0..*
     Address: 1

   meaning:
     ComAdr is assigned to exactly one Address at a time.



4. Candidate --- Address
   role: "Lives"
   multiplicity:
     Candidate: 1..*
     Address: 1

   type: normal association

   meaning:
     Candidate lives at one or more Addresses.
     Each Address belongs to exactly one Candidate.


5. Candidate --- CV
   type: qualified association
   multiplicity:
     Candidate: 1
     CV: 1..*

   qualifier:
     CV number (Name_Surname_number)

   meaning:
     Candidate has multiple CVs identified by a qualifier.


ADDITIONAL NOTES
----------------

- Automatic creation of reverse references required for each association.
- Allowed multiplicities: 1-* or *-*.
- Types of associations used:
  * normal association (Candidate-Address)
  * association with attribute (Company-(ComAdr)-Address) [implicit]
  * qualified association (CV-Candidate)
  * composition (Company-JobOffer)
